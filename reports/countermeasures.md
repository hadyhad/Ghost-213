#Four Golden Operational Security Rules
**Strict Access Control & Principle of Least Privilege
*Limit user and system access rights to only what is strictly necessary for operational tasks.

**Data Encryption at Rest and in Transit
*Encrypt all sensitive data both during transmission across networks and while stored in databases.

**Continuous Monitoring and Audit Logging
*Maintain comprehensive log monitoring to detect suspicious behavior and unauthorized access in real time.

**Regular Patch Management and Vulnerability Assessments
*Keep all security patches up to date and perform routine vulnerability assessments to mitigate security risks.
